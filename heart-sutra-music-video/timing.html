    Keys

    • Space play or pause
    • ← → 1 s back or forward, with Shift 5 s
    • + − zoom · drag empty space or shift-scroll to pan
    • F follow the playhead · L stop looping
    • 1 2 3 speed 1×, 0.75×, 0.5×

    Editing

    • Drag a syllable to move it, or its edge to move just that end. Syllables that touch move together; hold ⌥ while dragging to pull them apart.
    • Drag a kanji to move its syllables, or a line's thick bar to move the whole line (with ⌥, without moving when it's on screen). ⇧-click or ⇧-drag to select several syllables, then drag one of them.
    • Drag the ends of a line's thin bar, scene starts and handover ends, beats, section starts and passages of other singing. ⌥-click the section or other-singing lane to add one.
    • ⌥← ⌥→ nudge the selection 10 ms, with ⇧ 50 ms · Tab next syllable
    • J while it plays: the selected syllable starts now, and the next one is selected. Hold J to set its end too.
    • ⌘Z undo · ⇧⌘Z redo · C mark a line checked · K clicks · ⌫ delete a section · Esc deselect

    Where the timings come from

    • The lyrics were force-aligned to the song with two methods (Whisper large-v3 through stable-ts, and an MMS CTC aligner), each run on a separated vocal track and on the full mix. High confidence means they agreed within about 0.4 s. Scenes and their moments are anchored to those lyric times.
    • Edits are saved as you make them, with a log of every edit, in the page's own database (or, run locally, in this browser). Claude writes them back into the source files when you're done.
