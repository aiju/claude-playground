月見猫つきみねこ

Moon-Viewing Cat

Pixel art with no sprites in it. The temple, the maples and the 三毛猫 are a cloud of 3D Gaussian splats, redrawn every frame and snapped to a small palette. Drag across the picture to move the camera.

View 表示
Palette 色
Pixels 解像度
  1. Splat. Each Gaussian is a fuzzy 3D ellipsoid. Its covariance is projected through the camera into a 2D ellipse on the screen.
  2. Blend. Ellipses are painted back to front. Solid ones are cut off at half opacity so shapes get crisp pixel edges; sky, mist and lantern glow stay soft.
  3. Snap. Every pixel takes the nearest palette colour, with a 4×4 Bayer pattern deciding the ties. That is where the dithered sky comes from.
